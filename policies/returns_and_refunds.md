# NovaRetail Returns & Refunds Policy
- **Standard Return Window:** Customers may return eligible items within 30 calendar days of the delivery date.
- **Electronics Return Window:** Consumer electronics (headphones, smartwatches, tablets) have a strict 15-day return window from delivery and must include original packaging.
- **Final Sale Items:** Gift cards, perishable goods, and customized items are non-refundable.
- **Refund Processing:** Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
- **High-Value Refunds:** Refunds exceeding $100.00 require manual review by a senior support specialist.
