# NovaRetail Shipping, Billing & Account Policy
- **Standard Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days via ground carrier.
- **Express Shipping:** 1 to 2 business days.
- **Account Verification:** Customers must verify their identity via registered email or customer ID before any account changes or financial refunds are processed.
- **Fraud & Chargebacks:** Any disputed charges or suspected account takeover must be escalated immediately to a human fraud specialist.
